# Use of artificial intelligence in development

> Spanish version: [AI.md](AI.md). The Spanish version is authoritative. In case of conflict, the Spanish version prevails. [LICENSE](LICENSE) prevails over this document.

## Summary

Part of Cardumen Deck's code and documentation was developed with the assistance of artificial intelligence tools. The application **does not use artificial intelligence at runtime** and does not send prompts to artificial intelligence APIs.

Artificial intelligence assistance was used to accelerate development, review, and documentation. The maintainers are responsible for human review, testing, and the final decision on accepted code.

## How artificial intelligence was used

- Code generation and editing.
- Documentation drafting and review.
- Error analysis and improvement proposals.
- Configuration and workflow review.

Artificial intelligence does not replace human review or testing.

## What the application does not do

Cardumen Deck does not:

- call artificial intelligence models;
- send code, logs, or configuration to artificial intelligence APIs;
- use artificial intelligence to analyze services at runtime;
- include telemetry related to artificial intelligence.

## Limitations

Code or documentation assisted by artificial intelligence may contain errors, omissions, or suboptimal decisions. The result is not guaranteed to be complete, original, or fit for a specific purpose.

If you find a problem, report it through the repository channels. For vulnerabilities, use GitHub Security Advisories.

## Artificial intelligence-assisted contributions

If you contribute code or documentation assisted by artificial intelligence:

- you must review the result before submitting it;
- you must verify that licenses and provenance are compatible;
- you must not include proprietary, confidential, or unauthorized code;
- you must add or update tests where appropriate;
- you must describe the change and its impact;
- you are responsible for the content you submit.

Maintainers may reject contributions that do not meet these requirements.

## Confidentiality and internal policies

Do not use unapproved artificial intelligence tools to process confidential code, secrets, personal data, or company information. If you use Cardumen Deck on a company device or with company code, that company's internal policies may apply.

## Licenses and intellectual property

The use of artificial intelligence does not guarantee code originality or resolve intellectual property questions by itself. Contributors must verify the provenance of the material and the terms of the tools they use.
